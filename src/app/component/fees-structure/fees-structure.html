Fee Structure Overview

Transparent, Fair & Value-Driven Educational Investment

At RLT Shanthinikethan Matriculation Higher Secondary School, we are committed to maintaining a transparent and structured fee policy that supports high-quality education, innovative learning experiences, and holistic student development.

Our fee structure is carefully designed to provide students with access to modern classrooms, technology-enabled learning, experienced faculty, sports facilities, co-curricular activities, and comprehensive academic support services.

We believe that education is an investment in a child's future. Therefore, we strive to ensure that every fee component contributes directly to enhancing the overall learning experience and creating opportunities for academic and personal success.

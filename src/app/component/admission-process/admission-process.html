Documents Required

Essential Documents For Admission Processing

To ensure a smooth and hassle-free admission process, parents are requested to submit the following documents along with the completed application form. These documents help verify student details, academic records, and eligibility requirements.

Mandatory Documents

  • Student Birth Certificate
  • Passport Size Photographs (Student)
  • Parent / Guardian Photographs
  • Aadhaar Card Copy (Student)
  • Aadhaar Card Copy (Parent/Guardian)
  • Residential Address Proof

Academic Documents

  • Previous Academic Report Card
  • Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Conduct Certificate
  • Community Certificate (If Applicable)
  • Grade 10 Mark Sheet (For Higher Secondary Admission)
  • Migration Certificate (If Applicable)

Original documents must be presented for verification during the admission process. Photocopies submitted with the application form will be retained by the school for official records.

Admission Guidelines

Important Information For Parents & Students

To ensure a smooth and transparent admission process, parents and students are requested to carefully review the following guidelines before submitting their application.

Application Submission

Admission applications must be completed accurately and submitted along with all required documents within the specified timeline.

Document Verification

Original documents must be produced during verification. Incomplete or incorrect documentation may delay the admission process.

Student Assessment

Students may be required to attend an interaction session, assessment, or counseling process depending on the grade level.

Seat Availability

Admissions are subject to seat availability and fulfillment of the eligibility criteria prescribed by the school.

Fee Payment

Admission will be confirmed only after the prescribed fees are paid within the stipulated time period.

School Policies

Parents and students are expected to comply with all school policies, rules, regulations, and code of conduct.

Important Note

The school management reserves the right to accept or decline admission applications based on eligibility, seat availability, and compliance with school admission policies. All admission decisions made by the school shall be final.
